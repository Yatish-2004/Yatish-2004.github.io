Who I Am

About Me

I like watching what makes people stop scrolling.

Not the obvious things — the tone shift when someone gets excited, the joke that lands with a whole room, the reason one post gets ignored while another one doesn't. That instinct is what pulled me into social media, and it's what I've spent the last few years turning into a repeatable strategy.

At Scroll Mantra, I run end-to-end social strategy for brands across aviation, sportswear, live events, and renewable energy — Vietjet, T10 Sports, KD Amdavad Marathon, and Bharat Solar Shakti — across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp, X, and Snapchat.

The numbers are the proof: a 45.3% rise in Facebook reach, 40.3% growth in Instagram reach, and an 809% jump in YouTube views, month-on-month. But the numbers are the aftermath. The real work is understanding an audience well enough to know what they'll stop for — and watching closely enough afterward to know if you were right.

Results, Not Vanity Metrics

Every campaign is built around reach, retention, and engagement that moves the needle.

Curiosity as a Strategy
